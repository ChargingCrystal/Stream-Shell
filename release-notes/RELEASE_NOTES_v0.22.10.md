# Stream Shell 0.22.10 — Persistent provider backgrounds

Author: Sven Rieseler

Apply over 0.22.9. Reload the extension in opera:extensions and reopen Stream Shell. No additional native-host reinstall is required for this version.

- Preserve the last visible provider background on Landing and Dashboard, including Music, Mangayomi and custom providers, independently of playback restoration.

Background artwork is not bundled.
