Stream Shell background asset contract (0.15.4+)

Release ZIPs intentionally do not ship the large background PNG files.
Keep your existing assets/backgrounds folder when overlaying an update.

Required filenames:
- netflix_wide.png
- netflix_compact.png
- prime_wide.png
- prime_compact.png
- disney_wide.png
- disney_compact.png
- crunchyroll_wide.png
- crunchyroll_compact.png
- youtube_wide.png
- youtube_compact.png

Wide is used by the 32:9 shell. Compact is used by the 16:10 shell.
